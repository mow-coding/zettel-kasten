## Design correction

The first design proposed a third provider writer mirroring restore/offload.
The review established that `object_storage_preservation.py` (v0.4.13)
already is an exact-approval, always-dialog live PUT writer with the
create-only PUT, the whole-object GET re-hash, the manifest-bound call budget
and the crash matrix, and that formal adoption already owns the batch
`wom_uploaded` projection. v0.4.33 therefore composes those two; the
`wom_uploaded` location contract, the execution-receipt path and fields, and
the content-addressed key stay as v0.3 defined them so Doctor,
backup-evidence, the gating matcher, restore and offload accept the result
unchanged. Other refuted points (a no-op `--local-bytes-only`, an
unreachable tier ladder, execution markers, a receipt path the validators
reject, a HEAD-only skip) are resolved as decisions 4–8 of the decision log.
