# WOM-kit v0.4.67 — a blocked upload preview says why, and window-approved captures get an exact route

This answers beta letter 185. After a conversation's Git backup succeeded, the remote preservation preview of its 21 objets stopped with `writer_unavailable`, and nothing said which of several causes it was. The conversation's captures had been approved through a window, so they carried no conversation mark. Installing the tool alone does not change a customer archive.

## A blocked upload preview explains itself

`writer_unavailable` has exactly four causes, all about the store label and provider kind:

- the provider kind has no live transport;
- the label is not a safe label;
- no registration exists under the label;
- a registration exists but its facts differ.

The preview now names the category, says what it is not (a missing writer, another writer, a lock, a credential, the conversation, the ledger), says that nothing after the label was evaluated, and lists the registered labels. A label seen in the ledger is not necessarily the registered one.

The preview also separates two facts: a Git backup never holds objet bytes, and remote preservation has a state of its own.

## Captures approved through a window

A capture approved through a window, before a grant, carries no conversation mark. It is complete and valid, but `--this-session` cannot select it.

- `object-storage-scope-list --approval-id <id>` (repeatable) adds the objets captured under exactly those approvals, mark or not. The list is bound to verified capture receipts and claims. The result counts what it found and never echoes an id.
- When `--this-session` selects nothing, the upload refusal says how many captures carry no mark and gives this route.

Nothing is rewritten; the reviewed list is the correction.

## Boundaries

Verified with a synthetic archive and the real command. The customer's store labels and run are not verified.

## Install after publication

Use the exact public release asset after its hash and size are reported:

```powershell
$womBootstrapNonce = [guid]::NewGuid().ToString("N")
$womBootstrapRoot = Join-Path $env:LOCALAPPDATA "WOM\bootstrap-v0467-$womBootstrapNonce"
py -3.12 -m venv $womBootstrapRoot
$womBootstrapPython = (Get-Item -LiteralPath (Join-Path $womBootstrapRoot "Scripts\python.exe")).FullName
& $womBootstrapPython -m pip install "https://github.com/mow-coding/zettel-kasten/releases/download/v0.4.67/wom_kit-0.4.67-py3-none-any.whl"
& "$womBootstrapRoot\Scripts\archive.exe" --version
```

Require `archive 0.4.67` from a new process, then follow the reviewed `project-version-update` flow for an existing project.
