# v0.3.272 - Approval-Gated Interrupted Title Recovery

v0.3.272 adds the CLI-only single-case executor for fixed actions reviewed
through the v0.3.271 title-remap recovery plan.

## What Changed

- Added `archive zet-title-remap-recover` and alias
  `title-remap-recover`.
- Preview binds one content-free transaction-journal SHA-256, the complete
  current recovery-plan digest, and the exact fixed action.
- The only executable fixed actions are
  `cleanup_unstarted_title_transaction_evidence`,
  `rollback_uncommitted_title_apply_to_before`, and
  `cleanup_verified_completed_title_evidence`; `manual_forensic_hold`
  remains non-executable.
- Approval additionally requires a safe reviewer id,
  `--affirm-recovery-reviewed`, and `--affirm-archive-quiescent`.
- Added a cross-platform non-blocking recovery guard and fresh plan
  revalidation under that guard.
- Reacquires the common title write lock only when absent; invalid present
  locks and locks belonging to another retained case are preserved and
  blocked.
- Restores uncommitted after-hash participants only from already verified
  complete prior-byte snapshots, then verifies the whole batch at before
  hashes.
- Cleans prepared residue without canonical writes and cleans exactly verified
  stale-completed residue without changing the immutable receipt.
- Retains transaction evidence on any incomplete write or cleanup and requires
  a fresh plan and approval after interruption.

## Privacy And Scope

The executor echoes no title text/hash/length, body text, zet id/path,
proposal SHA, evidence path, reviewer id, provider value, secret, or absolute
local path. It exposes no MCP method and calls no provider or model.

It does not resume an uncommitted apply, create or finalize a receipt, delete
prior-byte snapshots, or revert a completed title change. Approved
completed-title revert remains a separate later release.
