# v0.3.275 - Read-Only Title Revert Recovery Plan

Date: 2026-07-28

WOM v0.3.275 adds the read-only recovery-decision rung for a title-remap
compensation left incomplete by a process kill, shutdown, or power
interruption.

## Added

- CLI-only `zet-title-remap-revert-recovery-plan` and alias
  `title-remap-revert-recovery-plan`.
- Complete bounded reuse of title apply/revert receipt, journal, snapshot,
  canonical-hash, and common-lock audit evidence.
- Four fixed non-executable decisions:
  `cleanup_unstarted_title_revert_transaction_evidence`,
  `resume_title_revert_forward_and_finalize_receipt`,
  `finalize_title_revert_receipt`, and
  `cleanup_verified_completed_title_revert_evidence`.
- `manual_forensic_hold` for divergent, malformed, unverified-receipt,
  invalid-snapshot, or unsafe-lock evidence.
- Content-free case SHA-256 and complete plan-digest binding for a later
  dedicated approval-gated executor.

## Safety Boundary

- `--dry-run` is mandatory.
- Every case reports `execution_implemented: false` and
  `safe_to_execute_now: false`.
- The older interrupted-apply recovery planner/executor remains closed to
  revert cases.
- A missing common lock is reported as a later reacquisition requirement; an
  invalid or orphaned lock is a forensic hold.
- Output withholds title text/hash/length, body text, zet ids/paths, reviewer
  ids, proposal hashes, evidence paths, provider values, secrets, and absolute
  local paths.
- No provider/model is called and no MCP method is exposed.

## Still Not Implemented

- Automatic title-revert hard-exit recovery.
- Approval-gated execution of any returned revert recovery action.
- Receipt finalization, canonical restoration, or evidence cleanup by this
  command.

Preserve all source/revert receipts, journals, locks, canonical participants,
and prior-byte snapshots.
