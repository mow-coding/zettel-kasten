# v0.3.276 - Approval-Gated Title Revert Recovery

Date: 2026-07-28

WOM v0.3.276 adds the CLI-only executor for one freshly reviewed fixed action
from the v0.3.275 title-revert recovery plan.

## Added

- `zet-title-remap-revert-recover` and alias
  `title-remap-revert-recover`.
- Read-only preview bound to one revert-journal SHA-256, the complete plan
  digest, and the exact fixed action.
- Approval gates requiring a safe reviewer id, recovery-reviewed affirmation,
  and archive-quiescent affirmation.
- Shared non-blocking serialization with the interrupted-apply recovery
  executor and complete plan regeneration under that guard.
- Safe execution for prepared cleanup, partial compensation continuation,
  missing compensation-receipt finalization, and verified stale-completed
  residue cleanup.
- Missing common-lock reacquisition with exclusive-create semantics.
- Hard-exit and cleanup-failure retry that requires a fresh plan and never
  moves already restored participants back to applied bytes.

## Safety And Privacy

- `manual_forensic_hold` remains non-executable.
- Every private receipt, journal, lock, snapshot, and canonical participant is
  revalidated before use.
- The original source apply receipt and prior-byte snapshots are preserved.
- Existing verified compensation receipts are preserved byte-for-byte.
- Output withholds titles, title hashes/lengths, bodies, zet ids/paths,
  proposal hashes, evidence paths, reviewer ids, provider values, secrets, and
  absolute local paths.
- No provider/model is called and no MCP method is exposed.

## Explicit Remaining Audit Boundary

The approved recovery reconstructs the exact original deterministic
compensation receipt and preserves its original authorization. The new
recovery reviewer and affirmations gate this execution but are not persisted
in a separate immutable receipt in v0.3.276. The result reports this explicitly
as `new_recovery_approval_persisted_in_separate_receipt: false`.
