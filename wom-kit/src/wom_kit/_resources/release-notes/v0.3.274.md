# v0.3.274 - Approved Completed Title Revert

Date: 2026-07-28

WOM v0.3.274 adds the approval-gated execution rung after the v0.3.273
completed-title revert plan.

## Added

- CLI-only `zet-title-remap-revert` and alias `title-remap-revert`.
- Dry-run preview bound to the exact immutable source-receipt SHA-256 and
  reviewed revert-plan digest.
- Approval gates for a safe reviewer, all-title-reversions review, and archive
  quiescence.
- Exact whole-file restoration from verified prior-byte snapshots.
- A separate immutable, title/body-text-free revert receipt.
- A private hash-only revert transaction journal published before the first
  canonical write.
- Ordinary-failure rollback to exact applied bytes.
- Archive-wide completed revert receipt validation, including independent
  revert-plan digest recomputation.
- Retained revert journal classification as `prepared`,
  `partially_reverted`, `fully_reverted_receipt_missing`, `divergent`, or
  `stale_completed`.

## Safety Boundary

- The original title-remap receipt and prior-byte snapshots are preserved.
- The common title-remap lock serializes apply, apply recovery, and revert.
- Under the lock, the complete plan is rerun; stale receipt, participant,
  snapshot, history audit, or plan evidence blocks.
- CLI output withholds title text/hash/length, body text, zet ids/paths,
  reviewer ids, proposal hashes, evidence paths, provider values, secrets, and
  absolute local paths.
- No provider/model is called and no MCP method is exposed.

## Still Not Implemented

- Automatic recovery after a hard exit.
- A read-only recovery decision specifically for a retained revert journal.
- An approval-gated hard-exit revert recovery executor.

Preserve a retained revert journal, common lock, source/revert receipts,
canonical participants, and prior-byte snapshots for the next recovery rung.
