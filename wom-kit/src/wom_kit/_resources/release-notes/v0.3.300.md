## Safety and truth boundaries

Every new mutation is CLI-only, explicitly approved, bounded, content-free in
ordinary output, and revalidated against a fresh plan SHA-256 under its lock.
Unknown markup is not deleted, relation types are not inferred into edges, and
stored locators do not prove live remote reachability.

Synthetic fixtures exercise 508-item batch planning and 3,514-zettel markup
planning. The release does not open or modify a beta tester's private corpus.
External host adoption and the reported edge-count discrepancy remain pending
external evidence rather than falsely reported local completions.
